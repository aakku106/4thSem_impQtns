# AI in 2 Days: The Emergency 24-Hour Pass Plan

**Goal:** Score 25-35 marks by mastering the absolute most critical, high-frequency topics.  
**Your Situation:** 2 days, 24 hours total, starting with only basic AI knowledge.  
**Strategy:** We will ignore most theory and focus entirely on the two most repeated numerical/procedural problems. These are mechanical, easy to score full marks on, and appear in almost every single exam paper.

## 🗓️ The 24-Hour Study Schedule

This schedule is intense but focuses your effort for maximum return.

### **Day 1: Core Long Questions (12 Hours)**

Your goal today is to become a machine at solving the two main long questions.

- **Morning (6 hours): Informed Search Mastery**
  - **Topics:** A\* Search and Greedy Best-First Search.
  - **Past Papers:** 2076, 2078, 2079, 2080, 2080_new, 2081, Model.
  - **[View Examples](./informed_search_examples.md)**
  - **What to learn:**
    1. How to draw a **trace table** for A\* search (`f(n) = g(n) + h(n)`).
    2. How to trace Greedy search (using `h(n)` only).
    3. How to find the final path and calculate the total cost.
  - **Action Plan:**
    - **(2 hours):** Study the example in `g5_soln.md` and the `informed_search_examples.md` file.
    - **(4 hours):** Practice by solving at least 3-4 graph problems from past papers or online. First, follow the solution, then solve them on your own.

- **Afternoon (6 hours): FOPL Resolution Mastery**
  - **Topics:** Converting English to FOPL, FOPL to CNF, and using Resolution.
  - **Past Papers:** 2076, 2078, 2080, 2080_new, 2081, Model.
  - **[View Examples](./fopl_resolution_example.md)**
  - **What to learn:**
    1. The 6-step process: FOPL → Eliminate Implications → Standardize Variables → Skolemize/Drop ∀ → List Clauses → Resolve.
    2. How to negate the conclusion.
    3. How to unify variables and find the empty clause (contradiction).
  - **Action Plan:**
    - **(2 hours):** Memorize the 6 steps from the `fopl_resolution_example.md` file.
    - **(4 hours):** Practice with 2-3 different logic problems. The pattern is always the same.

### **Day 2: High-Yield Short Questions & Review (12 Hours)**

- **Morning (6 hours): Short Question Crash Course**
  - You will not become an expert. The goal is to memorize key definitions and steps to grab 10-15 easy marks.
  - **[View Cheatsheet](./short_qtn_cheatsheet.md)**
  - **Action Plan (2 hours per topic):**
    1. **Agents and PEAS:**
       - Learn the PEAS framework. Be able to define it for any agent (e.g., vacuum cleaner, shopping assistant).
    2. **NLP Steps:**
       - Memorize the 5 steps of NLP (Lexical, Syntactic, Semantic, Discourse, Pragmatic).
    3. **Min-Max Algorithm:**
       - Learn how to back up values in a game tree to find the best move for the MAX player.

- **Afternoon (6 hours): Final Review & Practice**
  - **(3 hours):** Re-solve one A\* search problem from scratch.
  - **(3 hours):** Re-solve one FOPL resolution problem from scratch. This will lock in the steps and build confidence for the exam.
